Bayesian · Perception
COMP30271 · Portfolio 1 2026

Bayesian Programming for Perception

Implemented Körding et al.’s generative model of audiovisual localisation to reproduce the ventriloquist effect: the brain infers whether sight and sound share a cause, then fuses or separates them according to sensory reliability.

  • Generative model: Bernoulli causal structure (one source vs two), Gaussian spatial prior, and modality-specific noise so vision is more precise than audition — matching the Körding parameter regime.
  • Ideal observer: Posterior over common vs independent causes, then inverse-variance fusion when a common cause is likely. Partial integration appears when evidence is ambiguous.
  • 10-trial qualitative run: Segregation for large disparities, partial fusion when cues overlap, strong visual capture on clear C=1 trials, and occasional noise-driven misclassifications.
  • 1,000-trial metrics: Causal accuracy ~81.9% (sensitivity 83.5%, specificity 80.3%); visual MAE 1.73° vs auditory ~5.5°; auditory ventriloquist shift 2.55° vs visual 0.14°.

Role: Sole author of the generative model, experiment loop, evaluation, and write-up. The ideal observer was the provided inference module.

ROS2 · NAV2 · Gazebo
COMP30271 · Portfolio 2 2026

Navigation with ROS2 in Gazebo

Configured NAV2 on a simulated JetBot (differential drive, 2D LiDAR) in Gazebo, then compared global planners and local controllers in an office maze, plus a hospital speed–safety trade-off.

  • Stack: AMCL localisation, NavFn / Theta* / Smac 2D planners, DWB and Regulated Pure Pursuit controllers, behaviour-tree navigator, costmaps with inflation.
  • Planner comparison: All five configs reached the goal with no collisions. Fastest: NavFn + Regulated Pure Pursuit at 53.45s (22% faster than NavFn + DWB). Shortest path: Theta* + DWB at 10.33m (17% shorter).
  • Hospital robot: Tested 0.15, 0.26, and 0.45 m/s. Recommended 0.26 m/s / 1.0 rad/s — 4/5 safety, 66.30s, smooth motion. Unsafe threshold ~0.4 m/s (near collision at 0.45 m/s).
  • Limits: Docker CPU sat near 570% of 600% on my laptop, so RViz lagged during the five-test run. Local costmap was optional in this static maze but would matter with moving people.

Role: Sole author — launch/config, RViz goal tests, planner/controller comparison, and hospital recommendation.

10.33m Shortest (Theta*)

OctoMap · SLAM
COMP30271 · Portfolio 3 2026

Probabilistic 3D Mapping with OctoMap

Set out to feed RGB-D point clouds into octomap_server2, filter the ground plane, project a 2D occupancy grid, and drive Nav2 exploration in the maze. Initial mapping worked; full Nav2 integration did not.

  • What ran: TF tree (odom → base_link → camera_depth_frame) verified; OctoMap subscribed to /camera/depth/points; teleop produced coloured wall voxels; first ground-filter pass made the floor transparent.
  • What broke: After a full restart, the same ground-filter parameters left the floor solid across six rebuilds. Docker CPU near 600% made RViz voxels flicker, so it was hard to tell config error from lag.
  • Not finished: Unified launch, projected-map costmap wiring, and sequential autonomous goals. The report documents the intended architecture, the failures, and what I would do next (commit working configs, better hardware, smaller debug slices).

Role: Sole author of the mapping attempt, parameter tuning, and the write-up of limits and lessons.
